Overview

My work spans embedded targets and cloud backends, with a consistent focus on production-grade delivery under real-world constraints: deterministic timing, measurable latency and throughput, robustness, and maintainability. I build complete systems from low-level interfacing and runtime integration through ML-enabled perception and optimization pipelines, primarily in C/C++ and Python, and I take responsibility for performance, reliability, and lifecycle considerations across development, validation, and deployment.

Embedded systems engineering

I develop and integrate embedded software stacks where hardware constraints, real-time behavior, and field reliability are central. This includes platform-level work on real-time operating environments and the engineering needed to translate requirements into stable software interfaces, along with application-level pipelines that must remain deterministic under load. I have built low-level interfacing and synchronization for timing-sensitive acquisition and IO, and I routinely design multithreaded pipelines with careful synchronization, memory ownership, and buffering strategies so that latency and jitter stay within specification. Across these systems, I apply performance engineering end-to-end—profiling, bottleneck isolation, vectorization, priority tuning, and runtime stability work—and validate behavior through hands-on bring-up and debugging using in-circuit and lab instrumentation such as JTAG and logic analyzers.

Embedded perception and real-time ML pipelines

I build real-time perception pipelines for resource-constrained systems with an emphasis on measurable accuracy, predictable runtime behavior, and reliable integration into production constraints. My experience includes multi-modal camera pipeline integration with end-to-end timing control, as well as compute-intensive perception workloads such as object detection, pose estimation, reconstruction, and segmentation when appropriate to the product requirements. I integrate compact ML components using embedded runtimes such as TFLite and TVM and benchmark compute paths against optimized math and acceleration libraries to identify the best accuracy–latency tradeoffs. This work is supported by systematic evaluation and tuning practices that prioritize operational metrics—accuracy, latency, stability under load, and behavior under degraded inputs—so models and pipelines behave reliably outside of ideal lab conditions.

Machine learning, modelling, and optimization

I apply machine learning and mathematical modelling to capture physical-world behavior and improve decision-making in operational systems, with a strong emphasis on reliability and comparative evaluation. I have deployed and refined anomaly detection approaches across classical and deep methods, including Isolation Forest, Autoencoders, Deep SVDD, and LoOP, and I structure evaluation to surface robustness issues early (distribution shift, operational noise, and system-level confounders). I also design hybrid modelling approaches that combine first-principles dynamics with data-driven components, and I have developed model predictive control (MPC) frameworks that improve prediction fidelity and control responsiveness in constrained settings. Throughout, I prioritize clarity of assumptions, measurable error modes, and the operational tradeoffs required for deployment.

Cloud and fleet-scale integration

I build the connective tissue between embedded systems and scalable services, enabling monitoring, data pipelines, operational feedback loops, and observability. This includes device-to-cloud and inter-device communication patterns (including CAN- and MQTT-based approaches where appropriate), and the engineering discipline needed to keep interfaces robust in the field through diagnostics, telemetry, and fault visibility. I have also worked on scalable data ingestion and processing workflows, including large-scale ingestion and service-side evaluation loops that support continuous model and system improvement. My focus in cloud integration is to ensure that embedded constraints are reflected in service design, and that lifecycle needs—deployment readiness, updates, validation gates, and long-term maintainability— are treated as first-class engineering requirements.

Applied R&D across domains

I have delivered cross-domain systems that require both embedded discipline and ML/optimization depth, typically involving tight coupling between hardware realities, algorithmic choices, and deployment constraints. This includes embedded control and optimization pipelines in advanced manufacturing contexts, where parameter tuning, defect detection and correction, and maintenance forecasting are integrated into operational loops. It also includes portable medical imaging firmware and data pipelines, where synchronized acquisition and buffering must align with computationally efficient reconstruction methods. In addition, I have built large-scale retrieval and ranking systems with rigorous evaluation using metrics such as MAP, NDCG, Precision, MRR, and latency, complemented by clustering-based analysis workflows that improve interpretability and system tuning.

Imaging and embedded control foundations

Earlier work established strong fundamentals in real-time imaging pipelines and embedded control, combining algorithmic implementation with hardware-validated testing and instrumentation. I implemented enhancement and reconstruction methods such as deblurring, denoising, and resolution improvement in embedded C++ environments, and I developed practical imaging features including auto-focus, Z-stacking, stitching, segmentation, and spectral unmixing. This work also covered the embedded control software needed for stable operation—sensor parameter control, modulation, calibration routines, encoder integration, and closed-loop positioning—validated through detailed lab bring-up and debugging practices.

Ongoing research collaboration

Alongside industry delivery, I continue technical-lead-level collaboration on system design and architecture using modern optimization and learning approaches, including graph neural networks, deep reinforcement learning (e.g., A3C), and clustering-based methods used for modularization and product architecture.
